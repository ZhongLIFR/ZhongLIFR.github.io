About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Great Bay University and the University of Science and Technology of China, supervised by Prof. Zaiwen Wen and Prof. Dong Liu, and a guest researcher at Leiden University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Leiden in 2025, supervised by Prof. Matthijs van Leeuwen and Prof. Thomas Bäck. During my Ph.D., I visited the DAML group at TU Munich, hosted by Prof. Stephan Günnemann. I studied statistics at Tongji University and data science at ENSAI, France.

My current research explores generative AI for mathematical optimization, with an interest in both large language and multimodal models. My recent and ongoing work includes developing benchmarks to evaluate optimization modeling systems and investigating post-training methods, such as supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, to improve them. I am also interested in the trustworthiness of these systems, including robustness, explainability, privacy, and the detection and mitigation of hallucinations. More broadly, I am interested in how optimization modeling and solving can inform one another. As my research has gradually expanded toward these questions, I continue my Ph.D. line of work in trustworthy data mining, studying anomaly detection, graph learning, and subgroup discovery, as well as explainable and robust diffusion and flow-matching methods for tabular data. My doctoral research, Trustworthy Anomaly Detection for Smart Manufacturing, was part of the NWO Digital Twin programme and focused on predictive maintenance for lithography wafer-handling systems and high-end printers, in collaboration with ASML, VDL-ETG, and Canon. Before my Ph.D., I worked on lithography systems at Shanghai Micro Electronics Equipment and financial compliance analytics at HSBC France.

My work has appeared at NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, TKDE, TKDD, and DMKD, including a NeurIPS Spotlight, an AAAI oral presentation, and an ESI Highly Cited Paper. It has received 620+ Google Scholar citations (as of October 2026). I was selected for the Chinese Ministry of Education’s overseas postdoctoral talent programme in 2025 (approximately CNY 1 million in funding for 2025–2028) and received the Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (approximately CNY 250,000). I also serve as an Area Chair for the AI for Math Workshop at ICML 2026 and a Session Co-Chair at AAAI 2026.

Research Interests

  • Generative models: LLMs, multimodal models, diffusion and flow matching.
  • Data mining: anomaly detection, graph learning and subgroup discovery.
  • Applications: AI4Math, optimization modeling, smart manufacturing and healthcare.
